Privacy Policy

ShiftScan  ·  Last updated 7 August 2026

ShiftScan turns a work schedule into calendar events and alarms. It is built so that your schedule never leaves your phone. This policy sets out exactly what that means.

What stays on your device

Everything that identifies you or your work:

  • Photos of your schedule. The image is processed on your device and is never uploaded. We cannot see it.
  • Your schedule itself. Shift dates, times, codes, and the name or row you identified as yours.
  • Your calendar. ShiftScan asks for write-only calendar access. It can add the shifts you confirm. It cannot read your existing events, and it never does.
  • Your alarm rules.

There is no account, no sign-in, and no server holding your data. Nothing above is transmitted anywhere.

What is sent off the device

Two things, and neither contains your schedule.

1. Anonymous usage counts. ShiftScan reports which steps of the app are being used, so we can tell which parts work. This is the complete list of events:

scan_started · scan_parsed · commit_done · alarm_armed · paywall_shown · purchase_started · purchase_completed · purchase_cancelled · share_flow_shown · share_flow_completed

These are counts only. No shift times, no dates, no names, no image data, and no free text is attached to them. Alongside them, TelemetryDeck sends your device model and app version, so we can tell whether a problem affects one iPhone or one release. TelemetryDeck does not build user profiles and does not use advertising identifiers.

2. Purchase status. If you subscribe, RevenueCat processes the purchase and tells the app whether your subscription is active. Apple handles the payment itself, and we never see your payment details. Your subscription is tied to an anonymous identifier, not to a name or an email address.

What ShiftScan never does

  • No advertising, and no advertising software.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with third parties.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No location collection.
  • No reading of your existing calendar events.
  • No account, so there is nothing to breach or leak.

Sending us a schedule

If a schedule fails to read, ShiftScan may offer to let you send us the photo so we can improve how it reads that layout. This is optional, it always asks first, and it never happens on its own. If you choose to send it, the photo is stripped of location and other embedded metadata before it is handed to the iOS share sheet, and you choose where it goes. Declining changes nothing about how the app works.

Children

ShiftScan is intended for people of working age and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Because ShiftScan holds no account and no personal data on any server, there is no personal data for us to export, correct, or delete. Deleting the app removes your schedule and your alarm rules from your device. Shifts already written to your calendar stay there. They are your calendar's events, and you can delete them yourself at any time.

There is currently no switch inside the app to turn off the anonymous usage counts described above. They contain no schedule content and no identifier that points back to you. We would rather state that limitation plainly than imply a control that does not exist. Deleting the app stops them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes are noted in the app's release notes.
