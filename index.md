# ShiftScan Support

ShiftScan turns your posted work schedule into calendar events with wake alarms.
Everything runs on your phone — no account, no sign-up, and your schedule never leaves the device.

## Get help

Open an [issue](https://github.com/volat1/shiftscan-support/issues) and describe what happened.
Useful details: your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you expected versus what you saw.

**Please do not attach a photo of a real schedule** — it usually carries your colleagues' names.
Describe the layout instead, or blank the names first.

## Common questions

**A schedule wouldn't read.** ShiftScan handles some layouts better than others, and photos of
paper are harder than screenshots. Try a screenshot if your schedule is available digitally, and
get the whole grid in frame. If it still refuses, that's the app being honest rather than
guessing — it will not write shifts it isn't sure about.

**My alarm didn't ring through Silent.** Alarms ring through Silent and Focus only after you grant
Alarm access, which the app asks for the first time you set an alarm rule. Without it, alarms
follow your ringer settings. The alarm screen tells you which mode you're in.

**Shifts landed on the wrong dates.** Check the Dates sheet on the Review screen before
confirming. If the schedule doesn't print a year, ShiftScan works one out from the printed
weekdays and tells you when the result looks far off.
