ShiftScan

Photograph your posted work schedule. Your shifts go into your calendar, with a wake alarm before each one.

Everything runs on your iPhone. There is no account, no sign-up, and your schedule never leaves the device.

Get help

Email contact@newlifefhs.com and describe what happened. It helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what you expected to see.

Please do not send a photo of a real schedule. It usually carries your colleagues' names. Describe the layout instead, or cover the names first.

Common questions

A schedule would not read

ShiftScan handles some layouts better than others, and a photo of paper is harder to read than a screenshot. If your schedule is available on a screen, use a screenshot. Get the whole grid in frame. If the app still refuses, it is telling you it could not read the sheet rather than guessing. It will not write shifts it is unsure about.

My alarm did not ring through Silent

Alarms ring through Silent and Focus only after you grant Alarm access. The app asks for it the first time you set an alarm rule. Without that permission, alarms follow your ringer settings. The alarm screen tells you which of the two you are in.

Shifts landed on the wrong dates

Open the Dates sheet on the Review screen before you confirm. If your schedule does not print a year, ShiftScan works one out from the printed weekdays, and it tells you when the result lands further away than a posted schedule usually does.

How do I change which row is mine?

Your row is picked on each scan and shown on the Review screen. Tap a different row to change it before you confirm.
